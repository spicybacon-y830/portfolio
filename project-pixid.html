Pixid

As part of the team at Dafolle, I contributed to the visual identity for PIXID, the European leader in recruitment and temp-staffing software. The challenge was to translate a complex B2B platform — one juggling enterprises, agencies, and temp workers — into brand assets that felt as clear and reassuring as the product itself. I helped create a set of visual materials — slide decks, LinkedIn banners, and social posts — that carried PIXID’s clean, trustworthy tone across every touchpoint, giving a technical, security-first product a human and approachable face.
