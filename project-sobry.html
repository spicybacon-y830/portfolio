Sobry

Sobry aspires to lead an energetic revolution, embodying modernity, pragmatism, and liberation. The brand aims to break conventions without falling into startup clichés, establishing trust in a sector where credibility is paramount. The design direction must balance boldness with seriousness, innovation with realism, and disruption with clarity. The goal is to craft a visual identity that captivates and instills confidence in Sobry’s target personas. This identity should convey the brand’s seriousness, highlight its value proposition, and reflect a crucial equilibrium between audacity, seriousness, and sectoral innovation. The ultimate aim is to create an identity that harmonizes with their product.

Credits Luc Ousmane
