Chift

I designed the Series A pitch deck for Chift, a fintech platform that connects software products to over 100 European financial tools through a single unified API. A fundraising deck has to do a lot in a few slides — make dense, technical infrastructure legible to investors and build a confident case, fast. Working with the team at Dafolle, I shaped the deck’s layout, visual hierarchy, and slide system, carrying Chift’s clean, “one-click simple” brand into a story built to raise.
