Primo

Primo is an all-in-one IT platform that pulls device management, access, and procurement into one system. I designed its website — a deliberately minimal, almost clinical marketing site in the spirit of Framer or Hyperline, but bright and white. The goal was to make a dense, technical product feel effortless to understand: crisp typography, generous white space, and a restrained layout that guides the eye without decoration. Working with the team at Dafolle, I built a site that feels calm, precise, and trustworthy — the digital equivalent of a tool that just runs quietly in the background.
